About Us

Our story of luxury and hospitality

A Legacy of Excellence Since 1980

Founded by visionary hotelier Mr. Solomon Kerzner, BookMyHotel has grown from a single boutique property into one of India's most prestigious hospitality brands. Our commitment to unparalleled luxury and personalized service has earned us recognition worldwide.

Every detail — from the thread count of our linens to the curvature of our architecture — is meticulously crafted to create moments of wonder. We believe that hospitality is not just a service; it's an art form.

Our team of over 100 dedicated professionals ensures that every guest experience exceeds expectations. Whether you're here for business or leisure, we promise a stay that will be etched in your memory forever.
